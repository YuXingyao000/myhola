A natural photograph of an ordinary occupied office, with convincing material
texture, subtle surface variation and balanced photographic color. Soft highlight
transitions, realistic reflections and gentle contact shadows give the visible
surfaces a tactile presence. Natural local contrast reveals fine detail without
harsh sharpening. The environment is clean, maintained and quietly in everyday
use.

Visible oak laminate has fine directional grain and pores; carpet and upholstery
have delicate fibers. Painted furniture, matte plastic, glass, plaster and paper
each have their own characteristic sheen and roughness. Restrained handling
polish and wiped-surface variation suit the existing materials. The original
viewpoint, framing, arrangement, proportions and light direction remain intact.
Only surfaces already visible receive refinement, with the foreground component
unchanged and no additional objects, stains or lettering.
